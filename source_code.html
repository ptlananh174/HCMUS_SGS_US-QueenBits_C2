How it protects you

Each protection starts with what it means for you.

Your files never move

The AI travels to your local vault. Before it runs, the vault is checked to be running audited, open-source code, so a platform admin cannot quietly copy your files or add a scraper.

Data stays here→Model travels→Checked first
For the technically curious
Remote hardware attestation verifies the environment measurement against a published, audited build before any training job is released. Compute-to-data keeps raw files inside that boundary.
The AI cannot take more than it earned

Only encrypted learning updates leave. A proof shows they came from the approved training, not from copied files, so no one can build a shadow model.

Update made→Proof verified→Accepted
For the technically curious
Zero-knowledge gradient verification: the vault proves the update was computed by the approved training loop over committed data, without revealing the data.
No one can rewrite the rules alone

Changing a privacy rule needs 5 of 7 independent signers. No CEO, investor, or lead developer holds a master key.

Proposal→5 of 7 sign→Published
For the technically curious
Policy contracts are governed by a 5-of-7 threshold signature scheme across distinct role categories.
You can always leave

Each contribution trains its own isolated shard. Withdrawing removes that shard. Your key lives on your device, with 2-of-3 recovery.

Shard removed→Model rerouted
For the technically curious
Modular mixture-of-experts shards make deletion exact rather than dependent on “unlearning”. Keys use Shamir 2-of-3 secret sharing.

Questions

Is my data really never copied?
The design is that files stay in your vault and only verified, encrypted updates leave. In this prototype there is no real data at all.
What if I lose my phone?
Any two of your three recovery shares restore access. Recovery restores your control only.
Can my family withdraw for me?
No. Consent belongs to you. Family can hold a recovery share but cannot use it to share or withdraw on your behalf.
Can the company change the rules later?
Not alone. Rule changes need 5 of 7 independent signers.
Does withdrawing really remove the AI’s learning?
Your shard is removed and the model is rerouted. The overall model may become slightly less accurate, and the demo shows an illustrative amount.
What if a check fails?
Everything stops and nothing is shared. You can try again whenever you like.
Is this a legally binding contract?
No. This prototype is not a contract or legal advice. Any real service would need its own published terms.
What is actually real in this prototype?
The page, the state machine and the rule checks are real code. The cryptography, shards, votes and credits are simulated.
